Privacy Policy

Your privacy and trust are important to us. This privacy policy describes how MovieBox collects, processes, and protects your information.

Last Updated: August 22, 2026
Scope: MovieBox Mobile Application

1. Introduction

Welcome to MovieBox ("we", "our", or "the app"), a premium movie discovery and review application designed as your personal cinema companion. We respect the privacy of our users and are committed to protecting it through our compliance with this policy.

This privacy policy governs your access to and use of MovieBox and explains what types of information the app interacts with, how this data is utilized, and the protocols we follow to protect it. By installing and using MovieBox, you agree to the practices outlined in this document.

2. Information We Collect

We believe in data minimization. MovieBox is engineered to function seamlessly without requiring you to share personal information with us.

🔒 No Accounts Required

MovieBox does not require registration, user profiles, or accounts. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, calendar, or location data.

The only categories of information processed by the app are:

  • User-Generated Content: Your movie watchlists, favorite selections, and custom reviews are created and stored locally on your device. We have no backend server storing or listing these entries.
  • Technical Data: Standard connection metrics (such as your IP address, device model, operating system version, and system language) are processed temporarily by third-party APIs in order to complete search requests and retrieve movie details.

3. Third-Party Integrations

To provide high-quality movie assets, video trailer playback, dynamic app updates, and ad-supported services, MovieBox integrates third-party Software Development Kits (SDKs) and APIs. These third parties handle data according to their respective privacy policies:

The Movie Database (TMDB) Core API

Used to query metadata, posters, cast details, and ratings. Your device communicates directly with TMDB. They may log network metadata (IP addresses and timestamps) as part of standard server logs.

View TMDB Privacy Policy →
Google AdMob Advertising

Used to display banners, native, interstitial, and rewarded ads. AdMob collects mobile advertising identifiers (like IDFA on iOS and AAID on Android), IP address, and cookie/tracking indicators to deliver tailored advertising.

View Google Ad Policies →
Firebase Analytics Analytics

Used to measure ad performance, user interactions (e.g. ad completions, screen switches), and run A/B testing variants. It collects anonymous session details and does not store personal identity fields (PII).

View Firebase Privacy Terms →
YouTube Player SDK Media

Used to stream movie trailers directly inside the application. When you click play and load a video, YouTube (owned by Google) may track video playback metrics and device information.

View Google Privacy Policy →

4. Local Storage

MovieBox stores your user settings, movie watchlist, favorites, and reviews inside high-performance local database boxes on your device (utilizing Hive and Shared Preferences).

This data is never sent to our servers. Because your data lives strictly on your device:

  • We cannot read, restore, or export your watchlist, favorites, or reviews if you lose your phone.
  • If you clear the app's cache/storage or uninstall MovieBox from your device, all saved movies and custom data will be **permanently deleted**.

5. Data Sharing

Since we do not collect or own any personal data, we do not—and cannot—sell, trade, rent, or lease your personal information. Any data shared with third-party providers (such as Google AdMob or Firebase) is handled directly by their respective services for utility functions (like analytics and serving ads) in accordance with the consent you grant during app configuration.

6. Your Privacy Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Depending on your geographic location, you may have rights under regional legislation (such as the General Data Protection Regulation / GDPR in the European Union/UK, or the California Consumer Privacy Act / CCPA in the United States).

Because MovieBox does not map local data to personal identifiers, we cannot access, modify, or delete your watchlist or reviews. You hold absolute control over your local data. You may exercise your rights as follows:

  • Consent Management: Users in the EEA and UK are presented with a consent form via Google's UMP (User Messaging Platform) SDK. You can update or withdraw your advertising consent preferences at any time through the Settings screen in the app.
  • Data Deletion & Access: You can completely erase all data stored by MovieBox by uninstalling the app or clearing its data in your device's app settings menu.
  • Tracking Opt-Out: You can configure your mobile device settings to restrict personalized ads (e.g., "Limit Ad Tracking" on iOS or "Opt out of Ads Personalization" on Android).

7. Children's Privacy

MovieBox is designed for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Union).

To enforce safe discovery, the application implements strict parameters on all API queries: include_adult: false is set on all TMDB content searches to filter out adult-rated films. If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has provided personal information to a third-party service integrated into our app, please contact the respective third-party service provider or contact us at the address below.

8. Data Security

We deploy standard security protocols to safeguard data transmissions and app integrity:

  • HTTPS Enforced: All network requests made to third-party endpoints (TMDB, YouTube API, Firebase) are encrypted using TLS/HTTPS.
  • Code Obfuscation: Release builds of MovieBox are obfuscated and split to prevent reverse engineering and ensure security keys are protected.
  • Safe Storage: API configuration keys are loaded dynamically at runtime rather than being stored statically in source code.

9. Changes to This Policy

We reserves the right to modify this privacy policy at any time. If we make structural modifications to this policy, we will post the updated version here and display a notice within the Settings or Splash screen of the MovieBox application. We encourage you to periodically review this page to remain informed of our data practices.
